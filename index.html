OUR QUALITY PROCESS

A beautiful object should do more than look considered. Before a design is offered for sale, prototypes are intended to be examined, tested in realistic use, improved where needed, and retested. Everyday-use trials are for prototypes—not the individual products prepared for customers.

01

Materials & construction

We inspect the yarn, lining, structural layers, seams, edges and finishing. For knitted bags, the concept uses an outer knitted layer, a strong non-stretch textile layer for structure, and a soft teddy-style lining. The finished construction will be confirmed through prototyping.

02

Strength where it matters

For bags, we plan to check the base, seams, handles, chain and strap attachments, hidden fixings, and leather nameplate retention. Load testing will use a defined intended maximum load, with checks for stretching, movement, damage and loss of shape.

03

Wear, texture & finish

We examine whether the knit holds together, whether fibres shed, whether the inner textile tears or shifts, and whether the finish remains neat after handling. Other products receive checks suited to their purpose, including shape retention, stability, seams and surface finish where relevant.

04

Real-life use

After initial checks, a prototype may go through a planned one-week everyday-use trial with a volunteer tester. We record comfort, practicality, wear and any issues that appear during normal use. This trial supplements—not replaces—construction and strength checks. Products intended for customers are not used for these everyday trials.

05

Improve. Retest. Review.

If a prototype fails or needs improvement, we revise the design and repeat the relevant checks. Before a product launches, we also review its care guidance, intended use and final specifications. Each customer product then receives its own final quality inspection; it is not put through prototype-style everyday-use testing.

✳

Our promise is transparency. This is Brescia’s planned quality process, not a claim that every product has already been tested. We will share only accurate information about checks actually completed and any limits customers should know.

THE LAST LOOK BEFORE IT LEAVES US

Quality, in every detail.

FINISHED PRODUCT CHECKS

Every finished Brescia piece is intended to receive an individual final inspection before dispatch. These are quality checks—not everyday-use trials. The product prepared for a customer remains fresh and unworn; real-life testing is carried out on prototypes during design development.

01

Construction & finish

We look over stitching, seams, edges, shape and overall finish, checking for loose threads, visible defects or uneven details.

02

Materials & details

We inspect the knit, lining, decorative elements and other materials for visible flaws, damage or parts that do not sit as intended.

03

Function & stability

Where relevant, we check handles, straps, attachments, bases, closures and stability for the product’s intended use.

04

Final presentation

We check that the piece is clean, presentable and consistent with its description before it is prepared for dispatch.

✳

If something isn’t right, it isn’t ready. A piece that does not meet the applicable checks is held back for correction and reinspection. Products intended for sale are not used as everyday testers. The exact checks depend on the product, and we will not claim a check has been completed unless it has.
