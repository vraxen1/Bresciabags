THE THINKING BENEATH THE TEXTURE

Soft on the outside.
Structure within.

PROPOSED CONSTRUCTION

A Brescia bag should feel soft and tactile while keeping a considered silhouette. Our proposed construction pairs the signature knit with hidden stabilising layers and a supported base. This is a design direction under development; material choices, layer order and performance will be confirmed through prototype testing.

01
KNIT

The knitted exterior

The visible, chunky-knit surface gives each bag its warmth and character. It creates the texture, while the hidden structure helps manage stretching and shape.

SIGNATURE TEXTURE
02
FOAM

Flexible shape-retaining foam

A suitable thin bag-making foam stabiliser is planned to add body and gentle cushioning to the walls, helping the bag stand more neatly without feeling like a rigid box.

BODY & SOFT STRUCTURE
03
WOVEN

Anti-stretch structural fabric

A firm, non-stretch woven layer is intended to help the bag resist elongation and distortion. It also provides a more stable foundation for the handle and reinforced attachment points.

STABILITY & REINFORCEMENT
04
BASE

A supported, semi-rigid base

A thin plastic or purpose-made handbag base insert, enclosed safely between the structural layers and lining, is intended to help the bottom stay level instead of sagging into a bowl shape. Edges would need to be smooth, rounded and fully covered.

BOTTOM SHAPE SUPPORT
05
LINING

The soft inner lining

A planned plush or teddy-style lining conceals the internal construction and gives the inside a soft, finished feel. The base insert could sit in a dedicated pocket if testing shows that a removable design is practical.

COMFORT & FINISH
✳

Built in layers. Proven by prototypes. These are proposed design features, not verified performance claims. Prototypes will need checks for shape retention, weight, comfort, seam strength, handle stretch and how the base performs under the bag’s intended load. The final layer order may change to suit the selected materials.
